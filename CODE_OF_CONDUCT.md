# Community standards

Tailor Layer should be a useful place for security practitioners, agent developers, and first-time contributors to work together.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Make reports reproducible and protect private or sensitive information.
- Treat false-positive reports as product feedback, not adversarial behavior.
- Give contributors room to ask questions and revise their work.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when reviewing integrations or rules.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private data, and repeated disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users. Serious concerns can be reported privately through [GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/thepeternemec/tailor-layer/security/advisories/new).

By participating, you agree to follow these standards in repository discussions, reviews, and community spaces operated by Tailor Layer.
